# Pledge reports and analysis

## Pledge reports

CiviCRM comes with two reports for analysing pledges:

- **Pledge Detail Report** shows information per contact, including amount pledged, pledge status, next payment date, balance due, and total amount paid. It is based on the **Pledge Report** template.
- **Pledged But not Paid** lists pledges that are not completed and have a due date in the past. It is based on a template with the same name.

These reports are configured to display particular information. To suit your needs, you can create a new report from the relevant report template. See *Reporting Set-up* for details.

The **Pledge Summary Report** template can be used to create a report summarising total pledge amounts by, for example, city or month of initial pledge.
